Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your email address and password. Additionally, we store the expense data you enter (amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions) to enable the core functionality of the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve your experience with Spendly. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Maintain your user account and authentication.
  • Generate spending reports and summaries.
  • Improve app performance and user interface.

3. Data Storage

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored in a secure database. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party services for hosting or infrastructure, provided they adhere to strict confidentiality and security standards.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
